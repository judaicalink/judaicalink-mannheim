---
title: JudaicaLink Mannheim
---

The Mannheim platform of JudaicaLink: showcases and demonstrators of the work on the knowledge graph at Mannheim
Technical University, some of them accompanying research publications, and our current version of the graph.
What proves itself is offered to the production service in Frankfurt. Nothing here is a production service:
showcases may change or go away, and the endpoint is reloaded with every new version of the data.
